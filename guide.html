First: get yourself a sandbox

Best way to answer questions is to have the app open yourself. Go to seasoned.training (tap Manager sign-in), tap "New here? Set up your restaurant", make an account with your email, and name a practice restaurant (call it anything, "Jimmy Test" works). Now you have your own copy to poke at without touching theirs.

The two sides of the app

Staff side. No accounts. They enter the restaurant's join code and a first name, and they're training. The code and a scannable QR live on the manager's Setup tab.

Manager side. Email and password. The "Manager sign-in" link is on the staff side's Me tab (bottom bar). Managers see the whole team's scores and edit all content.

What staff see (the tab bar)

The staff side has four tabs along the bottom of the phone:

  • Tonight: the home screen. It greets them by name, then shows the 86 list as its own red block (names big, who called it), the specials with the quick check button, any assignments, and the training packs.
  • Lookup: the Rolodex. Type two letters, get the full card. Works offline. Underneath the search is a row of guest-preference chips (dry, sweet, oaky, light, bold, and so on): tap one and every wine, beer, or dish whose card says so comes up, so a server can answer "what's something oaky?" at the table. Chips only appear when a pack here actually has cards with those words, so the wine and beer starter packs are what light them up.
  • Progress: their day streak, badges, this week's leaderboard, shout-outs, then their stars per level with best percentage and time. Tapping a level starts it.
  • Me: their name and restaurant, the light/dark switch, the manager sign-in, and "switch restaurant."

Light and dark. The app is dark by default for staff. "Auto" follows the phone's own setting, so a manager whose phone runs light gets the light look automatically. The same switch is on your Setup tab. It's per device, so a bartender's choice never changes yours.

The bar hides during a quiz so nobody taps out of a round by accident. First-time staff get a three-line welcome on the home screen (Tonight, Training, Lookup) that goes away once they tap "Got it," and every empty screen says what it's for and what to do next.

The dashboard, tab by tab

  • Tonight: the daily board. Tonight's specials, what's 86'd, and a note to staff. The fastest form in the app; thirty seconds before service. The 86 list saves the moment you tap "86 it" or the ✕; the Post specials and note button is only for the specials and the note. The Share 86 list button drops the whole board into the staff group chat in one tap, for the phones that won't open the app. Lower down: "Today's 86 activity" (who 86'd what, when) and "Who can 86 items" (tick a name to let them call 86s from the floor). A red dot on the tab means a staff 86 came in since you last looked.
  • Content (opens first): the training packs. This is where editing happens.
  • Players: assignments up top ("+ New" posts "everyone passes Level 4 by Friday" to every phone, then shows who's done and who's dragging), and below that everyone on staff, one card per person, with best, average, percentage right, average time, and session counts. People who joined but haven't played show up too. Tap a card for their history and the Rename and Remove from staff buttons. People who were removed keep their old scores under "Former staff."
  • Recent: the latest completed levels, newest first.
  • Setup: who's signed in, the plan status, the light/dark switch, the staff join code with its printable QR and a New code button, and the POS sync card.

Editing content

Packs are quiz decks. Each carries a Live or Draft chip. Staff only ever see Live packs, so build quietly in Draft and publish when ready. You can unpublish any time; nothing is deleted.

  • New pack: start blank, or "New from template" to clone a starter. Besides the cocktail and kitchen samples there's an industry basics library (Wine 101, Beer Styles, Espresso Drinks) and Steps of Service for the front of house: ten universal procedures (the greet, running food, wine service, handling a complaint...) whose quiz asks "what comes next?" Clone it and rewrite the steps the house way.
  • Pack editor: title, icon, tagline, plus each level's name and lives. The six quiz mechanics themselves are fixed. Buttons at the bottom: Save, Publish, Make a Spanish copy, Delete.
  • The small heading above the title is the station. It shows on each pack's row under Training on the staff's home screen ("Bar Exam · 6 levels"), and packs with the same station sit together. Make up any station name you like.
  • Items: each recipe is an item. Tap to edit, arrows to reorder, ✕ to delete. An item needs a name and at least one ingredient with both an amount and a name.
  • Publishing an empty pack is blocked on purpose. Add at least one item first.

Getting a menu in (show this off)

Nobody should type recipes. In any pack there are three import buttons; all of them land in a review screen where you untick anything that came out wrong before it saves.

  • 📷 Add from photos. Photograph recipe cards, binder pages, even neat handwriting. Select a whole stack of photos at once and review them as a batch. This works for the service manual too (each procedure becomes a numbered-steps card and the pack becomes a Floor station automatically) and for a floor plan (each table becomes a card with its seats and section).
  • 📄 Import PDF / CSV. Upload a menu PDF or a spreadsheet export. Excel files need a Save As CSV first.
  • 🔗 Import from menu URL. Paste a link to their menu page and the app reads it. If a site draws its menu with scripts the app will say so; use the menu's PDF link or photos instead. One of the three always works.
  • Daily allowance. Each manager account gets 40 imports a day (every photo, PDF or link counts as one) and 10 Spanish copies. That covers a normal setup day; if a restaurant has a huge binder to load, tell Justin ahead of time.
  • Always review before publishing. Compare against the original.
  • Allergens especially. The app only copies allergens that are literally written on the source. It never guesses them, so if the card doesn't list allergens, the item won't have them. A person has to verify that information.
  • Level names set themselves. Import a food menu and the levels take kitchen names (Mise en Place, Portion Control...); a drink menu gets the bar names (House Pour, Measure Up...). If you've renamed levels yourself the app leaves them alone.

Spanish packs

Open any pack in the editor and tap Make a Spanish copy. About half a minute later a new draft appears: same icon and station, with the title, level names, ingredients, methods, and notes in Spanish. Cocktail and brand names stay as they are (an Old Fashioned is an Old Fashioned), amounts and units don't change, and each ingredient gets one consistent Spanish name so the quiz stays fair. Read it over, fix anything the kitchen says differently, then publish. The English pack is untouched, so a bilingual staff can pick either.

Streaks and badges (the competitive part)

A streak is days in a row with at least one level cleared, in the phone's own time zone. Badges are earned once and kept:

  • The Regular: a 7-day streak.
  • Dialed: clear any level without missing a question.
  • Passed the Bar: every level of a Bar Exam pack, nothing missed. Clean Line: the same for Line Check.
  • Safe Hands: an 86 It drill run with nothing missed.
  • Seasoned Professional: every published pack cleared plus a 14-day streak. Meant to be rare; it's the one the new hire wants next to their name.

When someone earns a badge, everyone sees it under Shout-outs on their Progress tab, and the Level Complete screen tells the earner right away. The weekly leaderboard is points since Monday from finished levels; a run that ran out of lives doesn't count. Two rules baked in: badges only celebrate (what someone gets wrong never shows up anywhere public), and the badge names are a proposal until Jimmy signs off.

The 86 It safety drill

Once at least four dishes have an Allergens section on their card, a third tile appears for staff automatically: ⛔ 86 It, an allergy fire drill. "Guest at table 12 has a fish allergy, which dish gets 86'd?" Wrong answers quote the real card, so it teaches. This is the owner pitch: games sell to staff, safety sells to owners. To light it up for a restaurant, make sure their dish cards carry allergen lines (verified by their chef, not by us).

How it decides what's "safe." The drill only ever calls a dish safe when its allergen line is clear. If a card says "verify," "may contain," "shared fryer," "see binder," or lists something the app doesn't recognize, that dish still counts as a problem dish but is never offered as the safe answer. So the cleanest thing a restaurant can do is write allergen lines plainly: "Gluten (flour), Dairy (butter)" or "None known." It reads the nine major allergens, including everyday words for them (aioli counts as egg, ghee as dairy, crawfish as shellfish, "contains nuts" as both peanut and tree nut).

The daily loop (this is the retention pitch)

Training is for week one. These three make the app something staff open every shift, and they run on the Tonight board:

  • The Tonight board. Manager posts specials, 86'd items, and a note. Staff see it as a card at the top of their screen when they open the app. Specials and the note expire after 24 hours on purpose (yesterday's board must never mislead tonight). 86s work on a service day instead: each one lasts until 4 AM, so a late close keeps its list, and the morning crew starts clean.
  • 86s save instantly and never get overwritten. Whether you, a server, or the POS calls it, each 86 saves the moment it's tapped and lands on the same list. Posting specials can't erase anyone's 86. Each one shows who called it and when ("called by Maria, 20 min ago").
  • Still out tomorrow? One tap. Anything 86'd before 4 AM disappears from staff phones and waits on your Tonight tab under "Still out from before today?" Tap Still out and it's back on tonight's list; tap ✕ and it's gone. Staff can do the same thing by 86'ing it again. Leftovers you ignore clean themselves up after three days. POS 86s are the exception: they stay until the register says the item is back.
  • 86 boxes search the menu. Start typing in either "86 it" box (yours on the Tonight tab, or the staff one) and matching dishes, drinks, and tonight's specials pop up from the training cards. Tap one and it's 86'd, spelled the way the card spells it. Typing something that isn't on a card still works.
  • Staff can 86 too (with your OK). At the bottom of the Tonight tab is "Who can 86 items." Tick a name and that person gets an "86 it" box on their Tonight card; the chip shows who called it ("Key Lime Pie · Maria"). Every staff 86 lands in "Today's 86 activity" on your Tonight tab, and a red dot on the tab tells you something new came in. Specials and the note stay manager-only. Phone push notifications are on the roadmap; for now the dot is the notification.
  • Tonight's quick check. Post the board and a 3-question mini-quiz appears for staff automatically: each special with a one-liner becomes a question, and if something's 86'd, the last question is "which of these do you NOT sell tonight?" Managers see who did it under Recent.
  • Assignments. Players tab, "+ New": pick a pack, one level or the whole pack, a due date, a note. It lands as a card on every phone with a Start button that drops them straight into that level. You see a count ("6/9"), a bar, and who's done versus who's dragging by name. Only a pass posted after the assignment counts, so "do it again this week" works. Close it when you're finished with it.
  • The Daily One (on standby). A one-question-a-day card with a streak counter. It's built and working, but it made the top of the home screen too busy, so it's switched off for now until we find it a better spot.

POS sync (Toast and Square, more later)

On the Setup tab there's a "POS 86 sync" card. Connect the restaurant's Toast or Square and the 86 list fills itself the moment something is 86'd at the register. What to know when selling it:

  • Toast needs the right plan. Instant sync requires Restaurant Management Suite Essentials or higher (their standard API access). If the restaurant isn't on it, no problem: the 86 list works with manual entry today, and our Toast partnership will remove the plan requirement later. Ask which plan they're on; their Toast rep can quote the add-on.
  • Square works on any Square account. The manager copies an access token and webhook key from Square's developer dashboard; the card walks them through it.
  • The setup is a paste. The card gives them one URL to paste into their POS and step-by-step instructions. Synced items show a 🔌 marker on the board.
  • First connection at any restaurant: loop Justin in. The last mile of every POS integration gets finished against a real account.

Trying the quizzes yourself

From the dashboard, tap the ← arrow (top left). You get the staff menu with every Live pack, playable as a manager preview: your plays are never recorded, drafts stay hidden, and the "Manager preview" strip at the top takes you back to the dashboard. Great for checking a pack before publishing, or for handing a prospect your phone at the table.

Quick answers to likely questions

"My staff sees 'code didn't match'"

Typo, almost always. The exact code is on the Setup tab. Easier: have them scan the QR there instead, it fills the code in.

"Staff can't see the new pack"

It's probably still Draft. Publish it, then have them close and reopen the app.

"It says training is paused"

That's the account plan, not a bug. Text Justin, he flips a switch.

"The app says offline"

It keeps working from saved content and uploads scores when signal returns. Nothing is lost.

"A bartender's stars disappeared"

Stars save per device. New phone means a fresh ladder (the Progress tab's best percentages come from the server, so those survive). Their score history on the manager side is unaffected.

"I forgot my manager password"

Tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen. A reset link lands in their email; have them open it on the phone they'll manage from.

"My streak reset and I played yesterday"

A streak counts days with a completed level, so a run that ended out of lives doesn't count. If they did finish one, check the phone's date and time zone; that's what the streak is measured in.

"Where's the Submit button on the multi-blank levels?"

Gone on purpose, so every level works the same way: tap an answer and it locks in. On levels with several blanks, you can change any pick until the last blank is filled; that last tap is the submit. The prompt says so.

"Where's the 86 It tile?"

It builds itself only when at least four dishes have an Allergens section on their cards. Add allergen lines (chef-verified) and it appears.

"What do the percentage and the time mean?"

Every run now records how many questions they got right (as a percentage) and how long the run took, next to the points score on Players and Recent. Points reward streaks inside a level; the percentage is the honest "do they know it" number. Runs from before Sep 8 show points only.

"Half my kitchen speaks Spanish"

Open the pack, tap "Make a Spanish copy," review the draft, publish. Both versions show on staff phones; they pick.

"How do I make everyone do Level 4 by Friday?"

Players tab, "+ New" under Assignments. Pick the pack and level, set Friday, post. Their phones show it with a Start button; you watch the names turn to checkmarks.

"A server wants to 86 things from the floor"

Tonight tab, scroll to "Who can 86 items," tick their name. They get the 86 box immediately; untick to take it away. You'll see every 86 they call in the activity list.

"Yesterday's specials vanished from the board"

By design. Specials and the note expire 24 hours after posting so nobody sells last night's fish. Post today's board.

"Our 86s from last night are gone"

By design: an 86 lasts until 4 AM so the morning crew doesn't skip a dish that came back in. If it's still out, open the Tonight tab and tap Still out under "Still out from before today?" (or anyone approved just 86's it again).

"Do I have to hit Post after I 86 something?"

No. The 86 saves the moment you tap. Post is only for the specials and the note.

"A bartender wants to look something up mid-shift"

The Lookup tab at the bottom of their screen. Type two letters, get the full card. Works offline.

"Can we get your logo for our staff room poster?"

Send the press link above. Tell them to use the horizontal lockup and pick on-light or on-dark to match their paper.

"Can I see it with real-looking data before I sign up?"

Join code 468FC7 on your own phone. That's The Copper Fig, our demo house; hand it over and let them tap around.

"Can I get the white version? Dark is hard to read in the office"

Setup tab, Appearance, tap Light. Or leave it on Auto and the app follows the phone. Staff have the same switch on their Me tab; it's per device.

"A guest asked for something oaky and the new server froze"

Lookup tab, tap the Oaky chip. Every wine whose card says so comes up with the line that says it. Only works if the wine cards carry tasting words, which the Wine 101 starter does.

"Where did the search box on the home screen go?"

It's the Lookup tab now, bottom bar. Same search, plus the preference chips.

"My staff say training is paused"

The free trial ended (or the account is paused). They can still use Lookup with what's saved on their phones, but nothing new loads until the restaurant subscribes. Tell Justin.

"Someone quit. How do I cut them off?"

Players tab, tap their card, Remove from staff. Their phone loses access right away. Then tap Get a new code (or New code on Setup) so they can't rejoin with the old one, and reprint the QR. Everyone else stays in.

"We have two people with the same name"

They show as two separate cards now. Tap one and Rename it ("Mike B."). The new name shows everywhere, including their past scores and the leaderboard.

"Should I change our code?"

If it's 6 characters, yes, once: newer codes are 8 characters and much harder to guess. Staff already in stay in; only new people need the new code. Don't change the demo restaurant's code (468FC7); it's printed in this guide.

"It says too many wrong codes"

Ten wrong codes in an hour locks that phone out for a bit. Read them the code off your Setup tab (dashes and lowercase are fine) and have them try again in a while.

"Import says it hit today's limit"

Each manager account gets 40 imports and 10 Spanish copies a day, and the count frees up over the next 24 hours. If a restaurant needs more on setup day, tell Justin.

"A new hire can't join; it says a lot of people just joined from this wifi"

The sign-up service limits how many new phones can join from one internet connection per hour. Have them switch to cell data for the join, or try again in a few minutes.

"The menu link import found nothing"

Some menu sites draw everything with scripts, so there's no text to read. Use the menu's PDF link or snap photos; those always work.

When to hand it to Justin

Anything about plans, billing, deleting a whole restaurant, account resets, or the app misbehaving in a way this page doesn't cover. You own "how do I use it", Justin owns "something's wrong with it".
