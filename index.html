mid-shift, mid-blank

Never guess on allergies

An allergy drill built from your own cards

"The guest at table 12 has a gluten allergy." Which dish has to stay off the plate? The drill uses only what your cards declare. If a card says "verify" or "shared fryer," that dish is never offered as the safe choice. When in doubt, the house rule is the same as yours: stop the plate and ask the chef.

Questions

Do my staff have to download an app?

No. It opens in their phone's browser from your QR code or a link, and they can add it to their home screen. No app store, no account, no password to forget.

We don't have our recipes written down.

Photograph whatever you have, even handwritten specs or a binder page, or upload your menu PDF. The cards come back for you to check before anyone sees them. Or start from our packs and edit.

Half my kitchen speaks Spanish.

Any pack can be copied into Spanish in one tap. You review the copy, publish it, and staff pick the language they want.

What does it cost after the trial?

$79 a month for each location, with every feature and unlimited staff. If the trial ends before you subscribe, staff see that training is paused, and nothing you built is lost.

What about the walk-in with no signal?

Once the app is open, it keeps working, and scores wait on the phone until it finds signal again.
