Day one · your restaurant

Pre-shift: read this out

About a minute. Have the poster up and the link already sent to the staff group chat.

“Quick one before we open. For the next month we're trying an app called Seasoned. It's our menu, our specs, and tonight's 86 list, on your phone.

Scan the code on the poster, or open the link I just sent to the group chat. Type your first name and you're in. No app to download, no password.

The first thing you'll see is tonight's board: the specials, what's 86'd, and anything I need you to know. Check it when you clock in.

When you have a few minutes, play Level 1 for your station. Anything you miss comes back so you learn it.

And the Lookup tab at the bottom finds any drink or dish from two letters, even in the walk-in with no signal.”

Manager checklist for day one

  • Posters up in the break room and by the time clock (Setup tab, Print a poster).
  • The link sent to the staff group chat (Setup tab, Send the link).
  • After pre-shift, the Players tab shows who joined. Fix a mistyped name with Rename.
  • Tonight's board posted before service: specials, 86s, and a note.
  • A first assignment: Players tab, + New, Level 1 of each station by the end of the week.
  • Optional: tick one or two trusted servers under "Who can 86 items" so they can call 86s from the floor.

Tip: tonight's board is what brings staff back every shift, so post it before service whenever you can.
